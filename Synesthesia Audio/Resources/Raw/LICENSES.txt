libraries that are not ours. The notices below are reproduced here, and in the app under
Settings -> About & Sources, as required by their licences.

MODIFICATIONS: the original file is not included. We distribute a derived, reduced work consisting
only of kanji -> romanisation pairs for personal names, with place names, organisation names,
product names and work titles removed, and one reading selected per kanji spelling. The derived
table keeps this licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The data is refreshed automatically; the creation date of the bundled copy is shown in
Settings -> About & Sources. Source data is updated by EDRDG; keeping it current is a condition of
the licence, and the update procedure is documented in README.md.
